# ADR-0013: Compliance is not certification

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-30

## Context

The system produces compliance-evidence reports. Overstating them as
certifications or audit opinions would be misleading and unsafe.

## Decision

- Reports are labeled as evidence of controls and findings.
- Limitations and untested conditions are always stated.
- No claim of certification, legal attestation, or audit opinion is made.

## Consequences

- Honest, defensible reporting.
- Users must engage qualified auditors for certification.
- Documentation and UI (if any) must reflect this distinction.

## Alternatives considered

1. Claiming compliance certification. Rejected: inaccurate and unsafe.
